    About Us

    Empowering communities through digital environmental education

    Building awareness for a sustainable future in vulnerable communities

    Our project is an Environmental Awareness Platform designed to reduce the information gap that affects vulnerable populations facing environmental challenges such as pollution and climate change.

    • Accessible content adapted to different literacy levels
    • Inclusive tools such as audio guides, subtitles, and simple visuals
    • Interactive learning through videos, eco-games, and infographics

    Our platform is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), ensuring that communities with limited technological resources can still access valuable environmental education. By combining technology, accessibility standards (WCAG), and social impact, we aim to transform environmental knowledge into everyday actions.

    How It Works

    Three simple steps. No complicated setup.

    1 Open

    Open EcoBridge on any phone or computer. Install it on your home screen to use it later without internet.

    2 Learn

    Watch short videos, play eco-games or listen to audio guides. Choose what works best for you.

    3 Act

    Try one simple eco habit every day, at home or with your neighbors, and see your community change.
